# Adding drivers and making changes

## Adding a named driver
- Anyone who drives your car should be named on your policy.
- You can add a driver online in Your Insurance Account with no admin fee.
- Adding a driver can increase your premium.
- The insurer may refuse to cover a driver because of their age, licence, residency or driving experience.

## Adding a temporary driver
- A temporary driver can be added for up to 28 days in each policy year.
- This is done by contacting Customer Service with your policy number, not online.
- An admin fee applies, plus any change in premium.

## Other changes you can make online
Through Your Insurance Account you can:
- report a change of address,
- tell the insurer you have changed your car,
- update personal details,
- view and download your policy documents,
- send documents securely, and
- accept or review your renewal.

Online changes have no admin fee, but the premium may go up or down.
