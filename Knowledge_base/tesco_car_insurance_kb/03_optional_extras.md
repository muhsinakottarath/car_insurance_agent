# Optional extras

Optional extras cost more on top of the main policy. The Clubcard 10% discount does not apply to them.

## Breakdown cover
- Provided by RAC Insurance Ltd.
- Four levels, ranging from roadside help to European cover for driving holidays.
- Breakdown call-outs do not affect your no claims discount.

## Upgraded courtesy car
- A courtesy car similar in size to your own, for up to 28 days, even if your car cannot be repaired.
- Included as standard on Gold cover.

## Key cover
- Underwritten by AA Underwriting Insurance Company Ltd; claims are handled by Keycare.
- Covers lost, stolen or broken keys, with a 24-hour helpline.
- After the first 14 days, it covers all insured keys, including house and business keys.
- Replacement keys and locks are covered up to £1,500 per policy, subject to terms.
- Key cover claims should be started with Keycare within 45 days.

## Motor Legal Protection
- Underwritten by AmTrust Specialty Limited.
- Covers legal costs up to £100,000 for pursuing a personal injury claim after an accident that was not your fault, where a successful outcome is more likely than not.
