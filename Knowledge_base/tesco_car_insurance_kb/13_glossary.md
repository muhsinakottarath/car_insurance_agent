# Car insurance glossary

- **Premium:** the price you pay for your insurance, yearly or monthly.
- **Excess:** the part of a claim you pay yourself. It is made up of a compulsory excess set by the insurer and any voluntary excess you choose.
- **Comprehensive cover:** covers damage to your own car as well as to other people and their property.
- **Third party, fire and theft:** covers other people and their property, plus your own car if it is stolen or catches fire, but not accidental damage to your own car.
- **Underwriter:** the insurance company that carries the risk and pays claims.
- **No claims discount (NCD):** a discount that grows with each claim-free year.
- **Protected NCD:** an option that keeps your discount after a limited number of claims, though your premium can still rise.
- **Fault claim:** a claim where the insurer cannot recover its costs from someone else, usually because you were responsible.
- **Policy schedule:** the document showing your personal details, car, cover level and excesses.
- **Certificate of Motor Insurance:** the legal proof that you are insured, listing who can drive and for what use.
- **Policy booklet:** the full terms and conditions, including what is and is not covered.
- **Courtesy car:** a temporary car provided while yours is being repaired after a claim.
- **Market value:** what your car was worth just before the loss, used when a car is written off.
- **Named driver:** another person listed on your policy who is allowed to drive your car.
- **Misfuelling:** putting the wrong fuel in your car, such as petrol in a diesel.
- **Cooling-off period:** a short period after buying when you can cancel and get a refund.
