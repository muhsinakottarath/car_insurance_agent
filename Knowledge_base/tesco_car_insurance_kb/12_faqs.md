# Frequently asked questions

**Who underwrites Tesco Car Insurance?**
Tesco Underwriting Ltd underwrites the main policy. Tesco Insurance arranges and administers it.

**Is there a discount for Clubcard members?**
Yes, a guaranteed 10% off the policy price. It does not apply to optional extras.

**Can I get a discount for insuring two cars?**
Yes. Clubcard members insuring two or more cars at the same address get a further 10%, but only when buying online directly.

**My windscreen is cracked. Will claiming affect my no claims discount?**
No, not if you claim through Autoglass on 0345 677 8888 and you have windscreen cover (Silver or Gold).

**I've broken down. Who do I call?**
RAC on 0800 003 001, open 24 hours. If you don't have breakdown cover, RAC can still help for a fee. Breakdowns don't affect your no claims discount.

**I've lost my car keys. What should I do?**
If you have Key Cover, call Keycare on 0345 366 0228 within 45 days. Without Key Cover, contact the claims line within 24 hours. Gold cover includes lost keys up to market value.

**I was hit by an uninsured driver. Will I lose my no claims discount?**
On Silver and Gold, the uninsured driver promise restores your no claims discount and refunds your excess once the details are verified.

**How long is my quote valid?**
Up to 30 days, or until your chosen start date.

**Can I let a friend borrow my car for a week?**
Yes, you can add a temporary driver for up to 28 days per policy year by contacting Customer Service. An admin fee and any premium change apply.

**Can I drive my car in Europe?**
Yes, UK residents' cover extends to EU countries for up to 90 days in a policy period.

**Where are my policy documents?**
In Your Insurance Account online, where you can view and download them.

**How do I cancel?**
See the cancellation document. Cancellation rights and refunds are in section 9.11 of the policy booklet.

**What if I'm unhappy with how my complaint was handled?**
After eight weeks, or after receiving a final response, you can go to the Financial Ombudsman Service. See the complaints document.
