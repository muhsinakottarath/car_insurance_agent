# No claims discount (NCD) and protection

## What NCD is
A no claims discount is a reduction in your premium for each year you hold insurance without making a claim. It does not grow in a year when you make a claim.

## Protecting your NCD
- You can protect your NCD if you have at least four years of NCD and no more than one fault claim in the last three years.
- With protection, your NCD is kept for up to two claims in the first policy year.
- At renewal, two claims within three years removes the protection, and a third claim in that period reduces your NCD.
- Protection keeps your discount, but it does not protect your premium. Your premium or excess may still rise after a claim.
- Protection terms can change at renewal; the policy booklet has the details.

## Without protection
Any claim where the insurer cannot recover its full costs from another party will reduce your NCD.

## Claims that don't affect NCD
- Windscreen and window claims made through Autoglass.
- Breakdown call-outs with RAC.
- On Silver and Gold, an accident caused by an uninsured driver, once verified (the uninsured driver promise).

## Using NCD from a company car
You may be able to use claim-free years from a company car if all of these apply:
- you were the main driver and used it for both personal and business trips,
- you were claim-free on it for at least one year,
- you no longer use it, and
- you provide proof on company-headed paper.
