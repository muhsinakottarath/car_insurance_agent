# Driving abroad and driving other cars

## Driving in Europe
- For UK residents, the UK cover level extends to any EU country for up to 90 days in a policy period.
- European breakdown help is only included if you choose the highest level of the optional RAC breakdown cover.

## Driving someone else's car
- Check section 5 of your Certificate of Motor Insurance to see whether you can drive other cars.
- Where allowed, cover is third party only, so damage to the car you are driving is not covered.
- You need the owner's permission.
- This is only available on Silver and Gold cover, for eligible customers.
- Full limitations are in the policy booklet.
