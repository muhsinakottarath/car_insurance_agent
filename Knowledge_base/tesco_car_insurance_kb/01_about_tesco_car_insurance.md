# About Tesco Car Insurance

## Who provides the policy
- Tesco Car Insurance is arranged and administered by Tesco Insurance.
- The car insurance policy itself is underwritten by Tesco Underwriting Ltd. The underwriter is the company that carries the risk and handles claims.
- Optional extras (breakdown, key cover, legal protection) are underwritten by separate partner insurers. See the optional extras document.

## What kind of cover is offered
- Three levels of cover: Bronze, Silver and Gold. All three are comprehensive cover.
- Third party, fire and theft cover is offered only at Silver level, and only for cars worth under £5,000.
- Separate products exist for electric and hybrid cars, and guides are published for older drivers and young drivers.

## Managing a policy
- Customers manage their policy online through "Your Insurance Account".
- In the account you can view and download documents, add drivers, report a home move or a change of car, update personal details, send documents securely, and accept or review your renewal.
- Changes made online carry no admin fee, although the premium may change.

## Claims record
- Tesco states that 99% of its car insurance claims were paid between January and December 2025.
