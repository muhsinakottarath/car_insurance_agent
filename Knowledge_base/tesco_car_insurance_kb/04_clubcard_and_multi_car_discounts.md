# Clubcard and Multi Car discounts

## Clubcard price
- Clubcard members get a guaranteed 10% off the policy price.
- The Clubcard must be in the policyholder's name and may need to be provided during the quote.
- The discount does not apply to optional extras or add-ons.
- Electric and hybrid drivers can also collect Clubcard points when charging at Tesco.

## Multi Car discount
- A further 10% for Clubcard members who insure at least two cars at the same address.
- Only available when buying online directly from Tesco Insurance. It is not available by phone, or through price comparison or cashback websites.
- The first policy must be in place at least 24 hours before the additional car is insured.
- Applied automatically at the start and at renewal while you remain eligible.
- Does not apply to optional extras, and minimum premiums apply.
- If you pay by direct debit, interest is calculated on the discounted premium.
- Tesco can change or withdraw the offer at any time.
