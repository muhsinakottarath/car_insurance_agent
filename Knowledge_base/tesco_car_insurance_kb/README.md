**Unofficial.** Not affiliated with or endorsed by Tesco. Every document is written in my own words from Tesco Insurance's public web pages, retrieved on 13 October 2026, and each one lists its source. Details may have changed since then.

## Ideas for test questions
- Does Bronze include windscreen cover? (No, it starts at Silver.)
- Am I covered if I put petrol in my diesel car? (Only on Gold, which includes misfuelling.)
- Can I get the Clubcard discount on breakdown cover? (No.)
- What is the weather like today? (Out of scope: the assistant should hand over or say it doesn't know.)
