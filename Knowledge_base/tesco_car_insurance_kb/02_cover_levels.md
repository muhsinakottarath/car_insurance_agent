# Cover levels: Bronze, Silver and Gold

Each level includes everything in the level below it.

## Bronze (comprehensive)
- Cover for your own car as well as other people, including fire and theft.
- A small courtesy car while your car is repaired after a claim (some exclusions apply).
- Replacement child car seat, up to £300.
- UK cover extended to EU countries for up to 90 days in a policy period, for UK residents.
- Emergency medical treatment, up to £100 per injured person.
- Personal accident cover up to £1,000 for serious injury to you or your partner while travelling in the car.
- 24-hour online accident reporting and a UK-based accident helpline open seven days a week.
- Audio, visual and electronic equipment fitted by the car manufacturer.

## Silver (adds to Bronze)
- Uninsured driver promise: if an uninsured driver hits your car, your no claims discount is restored and your excess refunded once the details are verified.
- Windscreen and window cover, including paint scratches caused by the broken glass.
- Audio, visual and electronic equipment, whether fitted by the manufacturer or added later.
- Personal belongings up to £200.
- Up to £150 towards getting to your destination or home after a valid claim.
- Silver is the only level offering third party, fire and theft cover, and only for cars worth under £5,000.

## Gold (adds to Silver)
- Misfuelling: draining and cleaning the fuel tank.
- Lost keys replaced up to market value.
- Enhanced courtesy car similar in size to your own (some exclusions apply). Upgraded courtesy car cover is included as standard.
- Child car seat replaced up to market value.
- Personal belongings up to £500.
- Personal accident cover up to £7,500.
- Up to £500 towards getting to your destination or home after a valid claim.

## Excess
- The comparison table shows minimum compulsory excesses of £200, £100 and £0 across the three levels, in the order Bronze, Silver, Gold.
- Your actual excess, including any voluntary excess, is shown in your policy schedule.

## Independent rating
- Gold cover is shown with a four-star Fairer Finance rating, and Silver with three stars.
