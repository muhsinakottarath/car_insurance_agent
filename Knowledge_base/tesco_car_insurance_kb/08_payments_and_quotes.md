# Payments and quotes

## Paying for your policy
- You can pay the premium in one annual payment or monthly.
- Monthly payment is by direct debit, and interest is charged on monthly payments.

## Getting a quote
- A quote stays valid for up to 30 days, or until the start date you chose, whichever comes first.
- A soft credit search is carried out with TransUnion when you ask for a quote. It doesn't affect your credit score at that stage, but if you choose to pay monthly, the search becomes visible to other lenders.
- Quote details may be kept for up to seven years.
- Clubcard members receive a 10% discount on the policy price (see the discounts document).

## Your data
Personal information may be shared within the Tesco Group, and with credit reference and fraud prevention agencies. The privacy notice gives the full details.
